About ACLyze AI

What is ACLyze AI?

ACLyze AI is an AI-powered platform that analyzes knee MRI scans to detect ACL tears and related injuries with high accuracy, helping doctors make faster, more informed decisions.

Who can use ACLyze AI?

ACLyze AI is designed for radiologists, orthopedic specialists, medical students, and researchers who work with MRI scans and ACL diagnostics.

How accurate is the diagnosis?

Our AI is trained on over 1,300 real MRI scans and verified by medical professionals, achieving accuracy levels comparable to expert radiologists.

Is ACLyze AI approved by health authorities?

ACLyze AI follows strict medical software development practices and is in the process of acquiring necessary certifications and regulatory approvals.

Getting Started

How do I upload MRI scans?

Simply sign in to your ACLyze AI account, go to the dashboard, and upload DICOM or JPG images of the MRI scan. The AI will process and analyze the data instantly.

Can I use it on mobile devices?

Yes, ACLyze AI is mobile-friendly. You can view results and patient history directly from your tablet or smartphone.

Are results stored securely?

All MRI scans and analysis results are encrypted and stored securely following HIPAA-compliant practices to protect patient confidentiality.

What file formats are supported?

ACLyze AI supports DICOM (.dcm) and common image formats like JPG and PNG for scan uploads.

Can I try it without creating an account?

Yes, we offer a limited guest demo mode where you can try ACLyze AI using sample scans.

Membership

Is ACLyze AI free to use?

We offer both free and premium plans. The free plan provides limited scans per month, while the premium plan includes unlimited access, priority processing, and advanced visualizations.

How can I subscribe to a plan?

You can subscribe through the ACLyze AI dashboard by selecting a plan and completing payment via Stripe. Plans can be paused or canceled anytime.

How do I manage my account?

Go to your profile settings to update personal info, manage subscription, view scan history, and download reports.

Do you offer institutional or team plans?

Yes, ACLyze AI provides custom plans for clinics, hospitals, and universities. Please contact us for bulk access or integration options.

Technical

What kind of AI is used in ACLyze?

ACLyze AI uses deep learning, specifically convolutional neural networks (CNNs), trained on labeled MRI datasets for accurate ACL tear detection.

Can I integrate ACLyze with my hospital system?

Yes, ACLyze offers APIs and integration options for hospital systems and PACS software. Contact us for details.

What happens if the AI gives a wrong result?

Users can flag incorrect results, which helps us improve our AI models. Our reports are intended to assist, not replace, medical professionals.
