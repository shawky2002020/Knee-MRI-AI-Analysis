About Us

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To empower healthcare professionals with AI tools that enhance accuracy, efficiency, and trust in musculoskeletal diagnostics.

Vision

To become the global standard for AI-driven ACL tear detection through continuous innovation and research.

Our Story

What started as a university research initiative grew into ACLyze AI — a powerful assistant for radiologists. Combining our full stack expertise with advanced AI mastery, we created a platform that transforms knee MRI interpretation, accelerates decisions, and supports healthcare professionals globally.

Our journey began with a simple question: "How can we make ACL tear diagnosis faster and more accurate?" Today, we're answering that question with cutting-edge technology and a passionate team.
